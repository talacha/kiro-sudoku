How to Play
  • Select: Tap/click a cell or use Arrow keys. Home/End jump to row start/end.
  • Enter digit: Tap a pad button or press 1–9.
  • Erase: Backspace, Delete, or 0.
  • Notes mode: N toggles mode; Shift+1–9 toggles a single note without switching modes.
  • Undo: Ctrl/Cmd+Z (last 20 actions).
  • Pause: P or Esc.
  • Mute: M.
